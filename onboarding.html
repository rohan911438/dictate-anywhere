🎙️ Dictate Anywhere

Press Ctrl + Shift + Space on any web page, speak, and your words are typed into whatever field has focus. Tap to toggle, or hold and release for push-to-talk.

  1. 1 · Allow the microphone

    Grant access now so your first dictation doesn’t stall on a permission prompt.

  2. 2 · Add your AssemblyAI API key

    Create one at assemblyai.com/dashboard/api-keys, then paste it into Options.

  3. 3 · Change the hotkey (optional)

    Manage it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

For long-form dictation, turn on Auto silence-chunking in Options — it sends a clip after each natural pause and keeps listening.
